Privacy and analytics

Who runs this website

MDServices is operated by Michal Dům. Contact me at info@mdoom.net with questions about privacy.

Visitor analytics

I load Google Analytics 4 only with your permission. Measurement covers page visits, basic browser and device information, traffic sources, approximate location and clicks on the email link or a project. An email click does not confirm that a message was sent.

Cookies and your choice

Google Analytics does not load without permission. This website stores your choice in your browser’s local storage for 180 days. Once allowed, Google Analytics may set its own cookies to distinguish visits. Advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled. Change your choice through Cookie settings in the footer. Rejecting analytics disables measurement and removes the analytics cookies this site sets on its domain.

Analytics provider

I use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google. Its data handling follows Google’s privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy. User and event data retention for this GA4 property is set to two months, without resetting that period on new activity.

Email contact

The contact link opens your email application. This website has no contact form and does not collect the content of your message itself. If you send me an email, I use it to respond to your enquiry and any resulting work.

Technical logs

The web server and entry proxy may record technical request information needed for operations and troubleshooting. These logs are separate from optional visitor analytics.
