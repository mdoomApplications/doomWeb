Custom applications built with AI

Need an internal tool or want to test an idea for a product? I help turn your brief into an application you can try in practice.

Discuss your project ↗

When a custom application makes sense

Perhaps your work depends on several spreadsheets, people enter the same data repeatedly, or standard software does not fit your process. We start with a specific task and the people who do it every day.

How I use AI in development

I use AI while designing and writing code. It helps shorten the path to a working first version. I verify the resulting code, application behaviour and connections to other systems. The brief and your operating environment guide the choice of technology.

What we agree on before starting

We clarify the main features, source data and users. We agree on the first version’s scope, pricing and handover. System integration and ongoing support can be included according to your needs.

Start with a smaller scope

A smaller tool or a first product version does not have to require hundreds of thousands of crowns. We can begin with a limited scope and add features as needed. Pricing depends on complexity, integrations and operating requirements.

Questions before we start

Can we start with a prototype?

Yes. We can first build a limited version to test a process or idea. We agree on what needs to work and what would still need development before everyday use.

How much will the application cost?

I propose a price based on scope. Features, connections to other systems and operating requirements all affect the cost. Tell me what the application should solve; you do not need a finished technical specification.
