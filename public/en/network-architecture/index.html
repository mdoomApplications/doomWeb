Network design and IT architecture

Network design needs to reflect how your business works. I help you understand the current environment and plan architecture around operations, security and future needs.

Discuss your project ↗

Understand the current infrastructure

We review the existing setup, the services you use and the problems you need to address. Application dependencies and the availability of individual infrastructure components also matter.

Design around actual requirements

We discuss access to systems, separation of network traffic and connections between locations or services. The specific design follows your environment and planned changes.

Plan changes around operations

We need to know which services must remain available and how to check the outcome. We clarify the scope of implementation and responsibilities in advance.

Technical consulting

If you already have a design or a specific problem, we can start with a review. Over 20 years in corporate IT have given me experience in understanding the links between networks, applications and business processes.

Questions before we start

Can I book just a consultation?

Yes. We can focus on a specific problem, reviewing a design or planning the next step.

What do you need for an initial discussion?

A short description of the situation and what you want to change is enough. A network diagram or documentation can help us clarify the brief later.
