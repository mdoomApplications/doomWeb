System integration and automation

When systems do not exchange data, people often bridge the gap. I help identify where a direct connection or an automated workflow would be useful.

Discuss your project ↗

Start with the existing process

We map where data comes from, who works with it and where it needs to go. We identify steps suitable for automation and decisions that should stay with a person.

APIs and data interfaces

The design depends on what the systems actually support. We check API availability, data formats and permissions. The approach also needs to account for a service being unavailable.

Automate repetitive work

Examples include passing form data to another system, transferring data on a schedule or preparing a report. We start with one specific workflow to check its usefulness and reliability.

Fit the solution into daily operations

We agree on checking results, handling errors and handover. My corporate IT experience helps me consider dependencies on other applications and infrastructure.

Questions before we start

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

We first look at the systems you already use. Integration options depend on their interfaces, permissions and available data.

Can we automate just part of a process?

Yes. We can start with one repetitive step and use the results to decide whether to expand it.
